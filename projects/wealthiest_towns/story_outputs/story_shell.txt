Story shell

More than half of households in Upper Makefield earned at least $200,000, making it the highest-ranked Bucks County municipality in a new analysis of 2024 American Community Survey five-year estimates.

Upper Makefield ranked 7th statewide, with 55.3% of households earning $200,000 or more. The municipality's estimated median household income range was $186,611 to $238,925.

Statewide, Ben Avon Heights in Allegheny County ranked No. 1, with 62.6% of households earning $200,000 or more.

Bucks County also ranked 2nd among Pennsylvania counties for estimated median household income. The county's estimated median household income was $114,764, with a margin-of-error range of $112,697 to $116,831.

Methodology note:
This analysis used 2024 American Community Survey five-year Data Profile estimates. Municipal rankings are based on DP03_0061PE, the estimated percentage of households earning $200,000 or more. Median household income ranges were calculated using DP03_0062E and DP03_0062M, the estimate and margin of error for median household income.
