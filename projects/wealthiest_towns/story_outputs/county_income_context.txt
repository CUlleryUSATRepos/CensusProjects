County median household income context

Bucks County ranked 2nd among Pennsylvania counties for estimated median household income in the 2024 American Community Survey five-year estimates.

Bucks County's estimated median household income was $114,764, with a margin-of-error range of $112,697 to $116,831.

The top five Pennsylvania counties by estimated median household income were:

2. Bucks County: $114,764 ($112,697 to $116,831)
3. Montgomery County: $113,915 ($112,010 to $115,820)
